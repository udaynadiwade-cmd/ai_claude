### The four readings of "Research Manager" — pick one

I need this more than I need any salary number, because it changes the whole kit:

| Reading | What they do | Where they come from | Band (4–7 yrs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Market & customer research** ← *what I built* | Sizing, competitors, pricing, buyer and lost-deal research | Kantar, Nielsen, Ipsos, Redseer, in-house insights | ₹8–15 LPA |
| **Business / investment research** | Feasibility, financial modelling, land and market due diligence | Anarock, Knight Frank, JLL, Liases Foras, PropEquity, IB/PE research | ₹10–20 LPA |
| **Data & insights / analytics** | SQL, dashboards, funnel and cohort analysis, experiment design | Product and D2C analytics teams | ₹10–18 LPA |
| **UX / product research** | User interviews, usability testing, journey mapping | Product companies with a design team | ₹12–22 LPA |

Bands are market ranges from current India salary data, not a recommendation —
they move materially with city and with whether you are hiring from an agency
(cheaper, better trained) or from industry (dearer, more commercial).

> **Note on seniority.** "Manager" spans ₹5 LPA to ₹30 LPA in India, and the
> title alone tells a candidate nothing. I wrote this at **4–7 years, hands-on**,
> because at 1–3 years you get someone who needs the research designed for them
> — which defeats the purpose when there is no one here to design it — and at
> 8+ you are paying for people management you do not yet have headcount for.
> If your budget is the same band as the two executive roles, tell me and I will
> re-pitch this as **Research Analyst / Senior Research Analyst**, which is an
> honest title for that money and will not waste strong candidates' time.

> **Note on notice period.** The 30-day rule from the other two roles will not
> hold here. Agency and corporate research roles routinely carry 60–90 day
> notice. Budget for that in your start-date planning rather than screening it
> out — it would eliminate most of the good pool.

> **Note on the hiring channel.** This role will not fill the way the other two
> do. Volume boards produce almost nothing at this level; direct LinkedIn
> outreach to a named list of research firms produces almost everything. The
> target list and the reasoning are in `pipeline/POSTING-PLAYBOOK.md`. Plan for
> your effort here to be twenty outreach messages, not five job postings.
