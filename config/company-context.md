## Company philosophy & name

Befach is an acronym for **B**etter, **F**aster, **CH**eaper. The "4x" in the
company's thinking means every product or offering should aim to be roughly
four times better, faster, and cheaper than existing market alternatives —
"four" signals the delta should be large enough that customers come to Befach
rather than Befach chasing customers.

This applies internally too: the way the team works should aim to do things
better, faster, and at a lower cost, and always look to use tools and methods
that increase productivity or customer satisfaction by 4x.

## Origin story

- **D'Cal water softener (2018)** — started with no capital and no clear
  plan, emerged from home experimentation, gained traction on Amazon then
  through distributors/dealers/offline sales nationally. Not a strong-quality
  product; recurring customer issues; couldn't be built into a proper brand
  due to no R&D background or investment.
- **Befach Rice (low-GI, diabetic-friendly rice, ~2020)** — added similarly,
  without a structured business-building process; couldn't be expanded into a
  full brand due to lack of food-industry background and focus.
- **By 2023**, reserves from D'Cal and Befach Rice funded a deliberate move
  into **import simplification for India** — matching Uday's supply
  chain/sourcing/logistics background (China, Indonesia, Malaysia) from prior
  corporate roles. Both founders prefer sourcing/procurement/distribution/
  marketing over manufacturing/R&D.
- The import business was validated via a Meta ad (no website yet) offering
  end-to-end import services: generated ~100 clients, mostly one-off sample
  orders, one client repeating 5–6 times. Built expertise in customs,
  logistics, and China sourcing. Team scaled to ~30 people across tech,
  sourcing, research, and marketing.
- **Mission** at the time: simplify international trade for India. **Vision:**
  a marketplace where buyers place orders for imported products and Befach
  handles end-to-end backend/fulfillment.
- **2023–2025** — struggled to operationalize smoothly: no proper website,
  inconsistent process, couldn't build an Alibaba-style catalog. Growth
  stalled.
- **Last 6–8 months** — AI tooling has made catalog-building much easier.
  Befach now has API access to Alibaba and 1688.com and can pull from other
  sourcing/dropshipping platforms; landed price is calculated systematically
  (China base price + customs duty + shipping + margin); catalog is live at
  **befach.com**; part of the tech team was recently replaced with more
  AI-savvy hires.
