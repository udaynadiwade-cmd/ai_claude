## Company Philosophy & Name

**Befach** is an acronym for **Better, Faster, Cheaper**. 

The "4X" principle: every product or offering should aim to be roughly **four times better, faster, and cheaper** than existing market alternatives — "four" signals the delta should be large enough that customers come to Befach rather than Befach chasing customers.

This philosophy applies internally too: the organization works to do things better, faster, and at lower cost, always seeking tools and methods that increase productivity or customer satisfaction by 4× times.

## Origin Story

### Phase 1: D'Cal Water Softener (2018)
- Started with no capital and no clear plan
- Emerged from home experimentation
- Gained traction on Amazon, then through distributors/dealers/offline sales nationally
- **Issue:** Not a strong-quality product; recurring customer issues; couldn't be built into a proper brand due to lack of R&D background/investment

### Phase 2: Befach Rice (Low-GI, Diabetic-Friendly Rice, ~2020)
- Added similarly without structured business-building process
- **Issue:** Couldn't expand into full brand due to lack of food-industry background and focus

### Phase 3: Import Simplification Business (2023–Present)
- By 2023, reserves from D'Cal and Befach Rice funded deliberate move into import simplification for India
- Matched Uday's supply chain/sourcing/logistics background from prior corporate roles
- Both founders prefer sourcing/procurement/distribution/marketing over manufacturing/R&D

#### Validation Phase (2023–2024)
- Validated via Meta ad (no website yet) offering end-to-end import services
- Generated ~100 clients, mostly one-off sample orders
- One client repeating 5–6 times; built expertise in customs, logistics, China sourcing
- Team scaled to ~30 people: tech, sourcing, research, marketing

#### Growth Challenge (2023–2025)
- Struggled to operationalize smoothly — no proper website, inconsistent process, couldn't build Alibaba-style catalog
- Growth stalled despite market potential

#### AI-Enabled Turnaround (Last 6–8 months)
- AI tooling made catalog-building much easier
- Befach now has API access to Alibaba and 1688.com
- Can pull from other sourcing/dropshipping platforms
- Landed price calculated systematically: China base price + customs duty + shipping + margin
- Catalog live at **befach.com**
- Recently replaced part of tech team with AI-savvy hires
