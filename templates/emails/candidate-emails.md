# Candidate Communication Templates

I draft these for you filled in per candidate. **I never send anything to a
candidate without you approving it first** — you always see the draft.

Placeholders: `{{Name}}`, `{{Role}}`, `{{Company}}`, `{{Date}}`, `{{Time}}`.

---

## 1. Acknowledgement — auto-sent by the Google Form

Paste into the form's confirmation message.

> Thanks for applying to `{{Company}}`.
>
> We've received your application. We review applications twice a week, and we
> reply to every single applicant — whether it's a yes or a no. You'll hear from
> us within 7 days.
>
> If you're shortlisted, the next step is a short phone call.
>
> — `{{Company}}`

## 3b. Practical exercise — Research Manager

**Subject:** `Next step — {{Role}} at {{Company}}`

> Hi `{{Name}}`,
>
> Good speaking with you. The next step is a short exercise. I know you have
> almost certainly done unpaid work for a hiring process before and I want to be
> straight about the scope: **two hours, hard stop.** If you are past two hours,
> send what you have and note where you ran out — how you prioritise under a
> constraint is part of what we are reading.
>
> Nothing you produce will be used by us. It is four short parts, and the last
> one is a single page.
>
> `{{Paste Parts A–D from screening/interview-kits.md}}`
>
> Could you send it back by `{{Date}}`? Any format — a doc is fine, no deck
> needed.
>
> One thing worth saying in advance: we are not scoring you on getting the
> "right" market number. We are scoring how you get to it and what you flag as
> uncertain.
>
> Best,
> `{{Your name}}`

> **Why the scope promise is in the first paragraph:** at 4–7 years, an
> unbounded take-home is the single most common reason a strong candidate exits
> a process — often silently, by simply not replying. Naming the limit and the
> "we will not use this" line up front costs you nothing and materially improves
> your completion rate in this pool.

## 5. Rejection — after application review

**Subject:** `Your application to {{Company}}`

> Hi `{{Name}}`,
>
> Thank you for applying for the `{{Role}}` role, and for the time you put into
> the application.
>
> We're not moving forward with your application this time. `{{One specific,
> honest reason — e.g. "We're prioritising candidates with direct experience
> selling loans or property, and that's where the shortlist landed."}}`
>
> This isn't a judgement on your ability — it's a fit call against a narrow brief.
> We'll keep your details on file and will reach out if something closer opens up.
>
> Wishing you the best with your search.
>
> `{{Your name}}`

> **Say something real.** A one-line specific reason costs you fifteen seconds
> and is the difference between a candidate who speaks well of you and one who
> does not. In a city-level hiring market, that reputation compounds — the sales
> candidate you reject today talks to the three you want to hire next quarter.

## 7. Offer

**Subject:** `Offer — {{Role}} at {{Company}}`

> Hi `{{Name}}`,
>
> We'd like you to join `{{Company}}` as `{{Role}}`. Everyone who met you was
> clear about it.
>
> **The offer:**
> - Fixed CTC: `{{amount}}` per annum
> - Incentive: `{{structure — be exact: % of what, calculated how, paid when}}`
> - Start date: `{{date}}`
> - Location: `{{city}}`, on-site, `{{days}}` per week
> - Reports to: `{{name}}`
>
> The formal letter is attached. Call me if anything is unclear, especially on
> the incentive — I'd rather over-explain it now than have you discover a
> surprise in month three.
>
> Can you let us know by `{{date}}`?
>
> `{{Your name}}`

> **On the incentive clause:** write it as a worked example, not a formula.
> "If you close ₹50 lakh of value in a month, your incentive is ₹X, paid with
> the following month's salary." One example prevents most disputes.

## 8. Keep-warm — for strong candidates you did not hire

Send 6–8 weeks later. Costs nothing, and this is where your second hire usually
comes from.

> Hi `{{Name}}`,
>
> You interviewed with us for `{{Role}}` a couple of months back and were close.
> `{{Are you still looking? / We may have something opening up soon.}}`
>
> Either way — how's it going?
>
> `{{Your name}}`
