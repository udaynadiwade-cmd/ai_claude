# Rubric — Sales Executive

**Read `befach-hiring-standard.md` first.** That file is the frame: Attitude 35 ·
Aptitude 35 · Communication 30. This file supplies only what is specific to
this role. It does not override the standard.

---

## Gate 0 — Hard filters (fail = Reject before scoring)

| Filter | Rule |
|---|---|
| Location | Cannot work on-site at our Banjara Hills, Hyderabad office and unwilling to relocate |
| Languages | Not fluent in at least Hindi, English, and Telugu — this is a core requirement, not a plus, for a consumer-facing D'Cal role |
| Compensation | Expected fixed CTC more than 25% above the top of our band (₹25,000/month) |
| Completeness | No CV, or the target-vs-achieved question blank / pure filler |

> **Note:** "no sales experience" is deliberately **not** a hard filter. Per the
> Befach standard, experience is not a pillar. Someone who has sold nothing
> formally but has run their own venture and closed customers can score well.

---

## APTITUDE — 35 pts (role-specific)

### Role skill — 20 pts

**Proven closing evidence — 12 pts**

| Evidence | Pts |
|---|---|
| States a specific target and specific achievement, numbers internally consistent | 10–12 |
| Numbers stated but vague, or achievement without target | 7–9 |
| Implies genuine quota ownership without figures | 4–6 |
| Generic claims only ("consistently exceeded targets") | 1–3 |
| No evidence of personally closing anything | 0 |

> Flag, don't reject: implausibly round numbers, or figures impossible for the
> stated deal size. Surface for Uday to probe.

**Category fit — 8 pts**

Befach sells **D'Cal, a physical hard-water-softener product, direct to
consumers** (B2C) — not to businesses. Score proximity to that motion: a real
product, a consumer buyer, in-person/phone close.

| Background | Pts |
|---|---|
| Direct consumer sales of a physical/installed product — water purifiers, solar, appliances, home improvement, security systems | 7–8 |
| High-ticket consultative B2C — loans, property, insurance. Multi-touch, trust-heavy, price-sensitive — same muscle | 6–7 |
| Other B2C, multi-touch (cars, education, healthcare, premium retail) | 4–5 |
| B2B or SME sales — the buyer differs but real closing ownership still counts | 2–4 |
| Low-ticket or transactional retail | 1–2 |
| No selling of any kind, including nothing self-initiated | 0 |

### Relevant education — 8 pts
No specific degree required for this role. Score on whether their education
gave them anything usable — commerce, business, marketing, or a demonstrated
self-taught equivalent. Never score institutional tier.

### Clarity and ability to think — 7 pts
Assessed properly in the roleplay. From the application: do they reason about
*why* deals closed or didn't, or just report activity?

---

## ATTITUDE — 35 pts

Score per `befach-hiring-standard.md`. Role-specific signals:

| Positive | Negative |
|---|---|
| High daily call volume, talks in conversion and closing terms | "Handled enquiries", "assisted customers" — passive language throughout |
| Asks about incentive structure — wants the upside | Only asks about fixed salary |
| Built their own lead sources rather than waiting for them | Blames leads, product or pricing for every shortfall |
| Has sold something of their own — any venture, any scale | No evidence of anything self-initiated |
| Back on the phone after a no | — |

**Churn note:** high job-change frequency is normal in loan and property sales.
Weight it lightly. Flag as an interview question, never a deduction.

---

## COMMUNICATION — 30 pts

Score per the standard. **For this role, communication is effectively the
product** — treat the score as provisional until the phone screen. Score
fluency across Hindi, English, and Telugu specifically; a candidate strong in
only one language is capped, regardless of how well they speak it.

- Cap at 22 without a voice sample, and say so explicitly.
- A submitted voice note that is clear, warm and well-paced can reach the top band.
- Written application quality is a weak but real proxy for spoken clarity.

---

## Bands and the override

Use the standard's bands. And the standard's override applies with force here:
**a candidate weak on sales aptitude but visibly 9/10 on attitude gets surfaced,
not rejected.** Selling is teachable; hunger is not.

## Assessment sequence

Phone screen → live customer roleplay (scored /50) → final conversation.
Full kit in `interview-kits.md`.
