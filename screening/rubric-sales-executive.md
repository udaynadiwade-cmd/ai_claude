# Screening Rubric — Sales Executive

100 points. I score every applicant against this and show you the evidence for
each score, so you can overrule me on any line.

## Gate 0 — Hard filters (fail = auto-Reject, no scoring)

A candidate is rejected before scoring only if they fail one of these:

| Filter | Rule |
|---|---|
| Location | Cannot work on-site in `[CITY]` and not willing to relocate |
| Experience | Zero target-carrying sales experience (pure support, backend, or unrelated) |
| Compensation | Expected fixed CTC more than 25% above the top of our band |
| Communication | Application text is unintelligible — for this role, written clarity is a weak but real proxy for spoken clarity |
| Completeness | No CV attached, or Q8 (target vs achieved) left blank or answered with pure filler |

Everyone else gets scored.

## Scoring

### 1. Proven closing track record — 30 pts
*The most predictive signal. Source: Q8, Q9, and the CV.*

| Evidence | Pts |
|---|---|
| States a specific target and a specific achievement, with numbers that are internally consistent | 25–30 |
| States numbers but vaguely, or achievement only with no target | 17–24 |
| Claims strong performance with no numbers, but role history implies genuine quota ownership | 10–16 |
| Generic claims only ("consistently exceeded targets") | 4–9 |
| No evidence of personally closing anything | 0–3 |

> Red flag to surface, not auto-reject: numbers that are implausibly round or
> impossibly high for the stated deal size. I will flag these for you to probe.

### 2. Relevant category experience — 20 pts
*You named this explicitly. Source: Q6, CV.*

| Background | Pts |
|---|---|
| Home loans, personal loans, LAP, real estate plots, or property | 18–20 |
| Insurance, mutual funds, or other high-ticket consumer finance | 14–17 |
| Other high-ticket B2C requiring multi-touch consultative selling (cars, education, healthcare, premium retail) | 10–13 |
| Low-ticket B2C or transactional retail | 5–9 |
| B2B only, or inside sales with no closing | 2–4 |
| Unrelated | 0–1 |

### 3. Communication quality — 20 pts
*The trait you weighted hardest. Source: written answers, voice note if submitted.*

| Evidence | Pts |
|---|---|
| Voice/video note submitted and it is clear, warm, confident, well-paced | 17–20 |
| No voice note, but written answers are articulate, structured and persuasive | 12–16 |
| Written answers are understandable but flat, terse or error-heavy | 6–11 |
| Hard to follow | 0–5 |

> I cap this at 16 without a voice sample and tell you so. The real assessment
> is the 10-minute phone screen — this score only decides who gets called.

### 4. Hunger and closer temperament — 15 pts
*Source: Q10 call volume, Q8 phrasing, job-hopping pattern, incentive-seeking language.*

| Signal | Pts |
|---|---|
| High daily call volume (60+), talks in terms of conversion and closing, asks about incentive structure | 12–15 |
| Solid activity levels, outcome-oriented language | 8–11 |
| Describes process and relationships but never outcomes | 4–7 |
| Passive language throughout ("handled enquiries", "assisted customers") | 0–3 |

### 5. Discipline and CRM hygiene — 10 pts
*Source: Q11, CV structure.*

| Evidence | Pts |
|---|---|
| Named a real CRM (Salesforce, Zoho, LeadSquared, HubSpot, Kylas) and used it daily | 8–10 |
| Spreadsheet-based but clearly systematic | 5–7 |
| No system, purely memory-driven | 2–4 |
| Evidence of disorganisation | 0–1 |

### 6. Stability and fit — 5 pts

| Evidence | Pts |
|---|---|
| Sensible tenure (12+ months per role), notice period ≤30 days, expectations within band | 4–5 |
| One short stint, explainable | 2–3 |
| Three or more roles under 10 months in the last 3 years | 0–1 |

> Judgment note: high churn is normal in loan and property sales. I weight it
> lightly and flag it as a question for interview rather than a reason to reject.

## Bands

| Score | Verdict | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 75–100 | **Interview** | Phone screen within 48 hours |
| 60–74 | **Strong maybe** | Phone screen if the top band is thin |
| 45–59 | **Maybe** | Hold. Revisit if the funnel dries up |
| Under 45 | **Reject** | Courteous decline — template ready |

## Bias guards I apply

- I do not score on college, degree tier, or English accent markers.
- I do not infer anything from name, gender, age, photo, or marital status —
  common on Indian CVs and irrelevant here.
- Employment gaps are noted neutrally as an interview question, never scored down.
- Candidates from unbranded or unknown employers are scored on their numbers,
  not their logo.
