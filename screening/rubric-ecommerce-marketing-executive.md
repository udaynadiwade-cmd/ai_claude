# Rubric — E-commerce Marketing Executive

Total 100 pts: **Aptitude 35 · Attitude 35 · Communication 30**

Same structure and bias guards as `rubric-sales-executive.md` and
`rubric-digital-marketing-executive.md`. Read `befach-hiring-standard.md` first.

**Score nothing on:** college tier, employer brand, name, gender, age, photo,
marital status.

---

## Gate 0 — Hard filters (fail = do not score)

| Filter | Rule |
|---|---|
| Location | Cannot work on-site in Hyderabad and unwilling to relocate |
| Hands-on gate | Has never personally logged into a seller panel and made changes — assisted-only, or watched someone else do it |
| Report-only | Experience is exclusively pulling reports or updating trackers on accounts someone else ran |
| Experience floor | Under ~1 year of actual marketplace work. Judge by what they did, not by job title or tenure |
| Completeness | No CV, or the "before / changed / after" question blank or pure filler |
| Compensation | Expected fixed CTC more than 25% above the top of our band — **band is `NEEDED`, so this filter cannot be applied yet** |

A candidate blocked at Gate 0 on **unconfirmed** information (e.g. location never
stated) is *not* a reject. Mark `Gate 0 blocked`, ask, and hold. Only a
confirmed fail is a reject — and rejections are always Uday's call.

## APTITUDE — 35 pts

### Marketplace operations — 12 pts

| Evidence | Pts |
|---|---|
| Owned catalogue on 2+ marketplaces; can describe a specific listing problem they diagnosed and fixed (suppression, buy-box loss, category rejection) | 10–12 |
| Owned catalogue on one marketplace competently, with specifics | 7–9 |
| Created listings but describes the work generically, no problems ever encountered | 4–6 |
| Assisted with listings only | 1–3 |
| None | 0 |

### Advertising — 12 pts

| Evidence | Pts |
|---|---|
| Personally ran Sponsored Products/Brands or PLA with real budget; states platform, monthly spend, and a before/after ACOS or ROAS credibly | 10–12 |
| Ran campaigns on one platform with some numbers attached | 7–9 |
| Ran campaigns but cannot produce a single number | 3–6 |
| Only monitored campaigns someone else built | 1–2 |
| None | 0 |

### Commercial numeracy — 11 pts

The differentiator for this role. Most candidates can list; few understand unit
economics.

| Evidence | Pts |
|---|---|
| Reasons unprompted about margin after commission, shipping, returns and ad spend; distinguishes ACOS from true contribution | 9–11 |
| Tracks ACOS/ROAS correctly but stops at revenue — no view of profit | 6–8 |
| Reports platform-dashboard numbers without interpreting them | 3–5 |
| Cannot explain how a sale becomes a profit | 0–2 |

**Probe:** *"You doubled revenue on Amazon last quarter. How would you tell me
whether that was actually good for the business?"* A strong answer reaches for
margin after all deductions without being led there.

---

## ATTITUDE — 35 pts

We are hiring someone to build a channel that barely exists. Ownership matters
more than polish.

| Positive | Negative |
|---|---|
| Set up a marketplace account from scratch — theirs, a friend's, a side venture | Only ever operated an account someone else built |
| Killed an ad campaign that wasn't working and can say why | Every campaign they describe was a success |
| Taught themselves a platform because the job needed it | Waited to be trained |
| Chased a suppressed listing or a stuck payout until it was resolved | Escalated it and considered the job done |
| Names the platform they are weakest on | Claims equal command of every marketplace on 18 months' experience |
| Curious about our products and margins | No questions about what we sell |

---

## COMMUNICATION — 30 pts

Uday's requirement is explicit: **smart, and very good at communication.** This
is weighted accordingly, and it is assessed on clarity of thinking, not accent,
vocabulary or fluency in English specifically.

| Evidence | Pts |
|---|---|
| Explains a campaign or a number so a non-specialist follows it; concise; volunteers the caveat without being asked | 25–30 |
| Clear and answers the question asked, but needs prompting for specifics | 18–24 |
| Understandable, but rambles, or answers a different question than the one asked | 11–17 |
| Hard to follow; jargon substituting for substance | 4–10 |
| Cannot hold a structured exchange | 0–3 |

**Assess on:** the written application answer, and how they handle one follow-up
question that requires them to explain a number.

## Bands

| Total | Action |
|---|---|
| 75+ | Interview |
| 60–74 | Keep for review — interview if the pipeline is thin |
| 45–59 | Weak — hold, do not reject unilaterally |
| < 45 | Recommend reject → batch for Uday's approval |

**Low-confidence rule:** a CV scored without the "before / changed / after"
answer is inherently low confidence. Record the score as provisional and say so
explicitly. Never present an inferred score as firm.

---

## Assessment sequence

1. Gate 0 — blocked or confirmed fail?
2. Score Aptitude from written evidence only
3. Score Attitude from evidence, not from self-description
4. Score Communication from the written answer; revise after any call
5. Write the verdict reason citing the specific line that produced each score
