# Ask before you write

Standing instruction from Uday, 2026-09-14: **always ask questions before
building a post.** No draft until he has answered.

Why: the aerospace post went through four rebuilds because the draft was
written before asking about his GE years, the MRO scope, HAL/GTRE/ISRO, and
who to tag. Three questions first would have produced the final version once.

## How to ask
- Numbered, so he can answer "1: ..., 2: ...". Same style as his decisions.
- Three to six questions. Short. One line of context each if needed.
- Ask what only he knows. Do not ask what the repo, `BEFACH-CONTEXT.md`, or a
  quick search can answer — find that first, then ask about the gaps.
- If he answers some and not others, draft with what he gave and mark the
  rest with `[[PLACEHOLDER]]` so `post.py` holds the send.

## Question bank (pick what fits, do not use all)
1. **Your angle.** What is your own experience with this? A year, a role, a
   moment, a number you remember.
2. **The one fact.** Is there a specific figure, name or example you want in
   the post that I would not know?
3. **Reader.** Who should this reach — importers, founders, aerospace
   vendors, hiring candidates? Any specific companies or people to tag?
4. **The ask.** After reading, what should they do: visit the site, DM you,
   compare a quote, nothing?
5. **Sell or no sell.** Is this one of the one-in-five posts that mentions
   befach.com, or a pure reflection?
6. **Off limits.** Anything not to say — a client, a number, a claim that is
   not ready, a name.
7. **Media.** Is there a video, screenshot or article to build on? Paste it;
   links to LinkedIn, claude.ai shares and most news sites do not open here.
8. **Tone check.** Any line in the last post that did not sound like you?

## After the answers
Draft, then show the draft once. Do not iterate silently. If a fact came from
a search rather than from him, say which line, so he can catch it.
