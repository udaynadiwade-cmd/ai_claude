# Voice — how Uday writes on LinkedIn

Author: Uday Nadiwade, co-founder of Befach (Hyderabad). Supply chain, sourcing,
logistics, Six Sigma Master Black Belt. Sourced in China, Indonesia, Malaysia in
a corporate life before this. Now building an import-simplification marketplace
for India (befach.com) on top of two older businesses: D'Cal water softeners and
Befach low-GI rice. ~19 people, ₹13.5 Cr revenue, aiming at ₹1,000 Cr in 3–4 years.

## What he sounds like
- **Operator, not influencer.** Writes from what happened this week: a
  shipment, a hire, a customs surprise, a tool that saved an evening.
- **Numbers over adjectives.** "Landed cost = China price + duty + freight +
  margin" beats "we optimise pricing". Real figures where he can share them;
  never invented ones.
- **Prioritised thinking.** He sequences: first, second, third. Posts often
  have that shape.
- **Honest about the misses.** D'Cal was not a strong product. Rice never became
  a brand. 2023–25 growth stalled. He says so. That is the credibility.
- **Better. Faster. Cheaper. ×4.** The Befach idea: an offer has to be roughly
  four times better to pull customers instead of chasing them. Use sparingly,
  as a lens, not a slogan.
- **Indian B2B reality.** Small importers, dealers, agencies, WhatsApp as the
  real channel, MSME cash cycles. Speaks to founders and operators like him.
- **Plain English.** Short sentences. No "delighted to announce", no
  "game-changer", no "unlock". No emoji strings. Hashtags: 0–3, at the end,
  only if genuinely useful.

## Shape of a good post
1. Hook line: a concrete claim, number, or contradiction. Under 12 words.
2. Line 2: the reason to click "see more".
3. Body: one idea, 80–200 words, paragraphs of 1–2 lines.
4. Close: a question he actually wants answered, or a flat statement. No CTA
   to "follow for more".

## Formatting on LinkedIn
LinkedIn renders no markup. `**bold**`, `_italic_` and `#` headings post as
literal characters, so `post.py` strips them. To emphasise, give the line its
own paragraph. Every newline is rendered literally, so drafts are unwrapped
before sending; price tables survive because a currency figure followed by a
dash is treated as a row.

## Never
- Never invent revenue, client names, or results. If a number is uncertain,
  write the post without it.
- Never post about a candidate, an employee's performance, or a pending deal.
- Never sell in every post. Roughly one in five can mention befach.com. On
  those, sell by showing a number only Befach can show, then invite the reader
  to check their own. Never "DM me", never "limited stock", never a price
  claim that is not on the site.
- Never publish without Uday's Y.
