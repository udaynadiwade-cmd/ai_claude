We spent two years importing badly before we built anything.

So every feature on befach.com is a scar. Here is what put each one there.

You see the landed price, not the China price.

We priced off supplier quotes for a year and kept wondering where the margin
went. On a unit listed at ₹6,028, duties, taxes and freight to the door add
₹4,417. That is 73% on top. You should see it before you decide, not after.

Minimum order is often one piece.

We bought containers of things we had never held. Order a sample instead. Put
several samples in one basket and you pay one freight for the box, not one per
product.

Every listing shows the same four facts.

Price, minimum order, delivery window, unit weight. Side by side against
similar products. You cannot compare honestly when each listing tells you a
different subset.

A delivery date, not a lead time.

Order today, arrives 6–11 October, customs cleared, to your door. "Twenty-two
to twenty-seven days" was never an answer anyone could plan around.

A GST invoice, and money back.

Photos and an inspection report before dispatch. Full refund if it does not
ship, or arrives damaged or not as described. And you can pay on UPI, which
should not still feel remarkable for a China import, but does.

None of this is clever. It is just the list of things that cost us money to
learn, turned into software so the next person skips the tuition.
