The AI smart glasses cost [[EXW]] in Shenzhen.

Nobody argues about that number. Everybody argues about that number.

Here is what actually decides whether you make money on them:

Supplier price, ex-works: [[EXW]]
Customs duty: [[DUTY]]
Freight, clearing, last mile: [[FREIGHT]]
Landed cost in India: [[LANDED]]

Four lines. Importers negotiate line one for three weeks and accept lines two
and three as weather.

They are not weather.

Line two is a classification argument. Smart glasses are a genuinely contested
category — eyewear, or consumer electronics, or a wearable computer. The HS code
you land on moves the duty materially, and you either win that argument at your
desk with documentation or you lose it at the port with a clearing agent who has
forty other files.

Line three moves with the lane and the season, not with your launch calendar.

The reason every product on our catalog shows a landed price and not a China
price is not that it is a nice feature. It is that the China price is not
information. It is one input out of four, and on this SKU it is [[SHARE]] of the
real cost.

If you are importing right now: do you know your landed cost on your top SKU to
the rupee, or do you know your supplier's quote and a rough idea?
