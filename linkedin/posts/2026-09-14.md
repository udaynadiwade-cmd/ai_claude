# Most first-time importers price off the China quote. That is the whole mistake.

pillar: Import & sourcing craft
status: draft

Most first-time importers price off the China quote.

That number is maybe half of what the product actually costs you by the time it
is sitting in your warehouse.

The quote is one line. The landed cost is four:

1. The supplier price. The only one anybody negotiates.
2. Customs duty. Decided by your HS code, not by your invoice. Get the code
   wrong and you argue about it at the port, not at your desk.
3. Freight and clearing. Moves with the season and the lane, not with your plan.
4. Your margin. What is left, and it is always less than the spreadsheet said.

We spent the first year learning this the expensive way, one shipment at a time.
Now every product on our catalog carries a landed price, not a China price,
before anybody is allowed to quote it to a customer.

If you are importing and you cannot say your landed cost to the rupee, you are
not running a margin. You are hoping for one.
