We put a PCB on the catalog last week.

The China price was the smallest number on the sheet.

Here is the whole sheet, because nobody shows you this one:

Supplier price, ex-works China: [[EXW]]
Customs duty, at the HS code this part actually falls under: [[DUTY]]
Freight, clearing and last mile to the buyer: [[FREIGHT]]
Landed cost: [[LANDED]]

The quote a first-time importer works from is line one. The number that decides
whether they have a business is line four. On this part the gap is [[MULTIPLE]].

Two things move that gap, and neither is the supplier negotiation everybody
obsesses over. The HS code, which is a classification argument you either win at
your desk or lose at the port. And the lane, which moves with the season whether
or not your pricing does.

This is why every product on befach.com carries a landed price before anyone is
allowed to quote it. Not because it is a nice feature. Because the China price
is not information. It is the first of four.

If you import: can you say your landed cost on your top SKU, to the rupee,
right now?
