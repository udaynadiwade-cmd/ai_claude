What separates them is not the price line. It is weight, minimum order, lead
time, and whether the factory is holding stock or building yours from scratch.

That procurement line is 73% on top of the factory price. It scales with
weight and lane, not with price — which is exactly why the ₹354 piece is
rarely the cheap one by the time it reaches you.
