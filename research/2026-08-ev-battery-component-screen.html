The third option

EV components, narrowed to what actually clears the bar: battery-pack and component distribution to 3-wheeler fleet operators and repair shops in Hyderabad — not the battery-swap infrastructure business, which is a different model already contested by funded players.

Why Hyderabad specifically — a real local catalyst, same shape as electricals

The finding that changes this from "interesting nationally" to "start here"

Telangana is explicitly positioning itself as India's EV and Energy Storage Systems manufacturing hub by 2030 — 1,600 acres of dedicated industrial land near Hyderabad city and the airport, already home to Mahindra, Olectra, Mytrah. Fact And specifically for the segment with the strongest replacement-demand signal: Telangana's EV policy exempts the first 20,000 electric three-wheelers from road tax and registration entirely, plus a 15% purchase subsidy. Fact This is a named, funded, currently-running local demand driver — the same structural advantage that made electricals the #2 pick, now on a third category.

The honest risk, unchanged from the first pass

What doesn't improve with better data

No stable universal spec exists yet the way ISO bearings have one. Battery chemistry, pack form-factor and BMS compatibility vary by vehicle platform and are still evolving — closer to the auto-aftermarket fitment-complexity problem already flagged in this series (scored 7/10, not 8/10, for exactly this reason) than to bearings' clean fungibility. This doesn't disqualify the category, but it means execution is harder than bearings even with the market size and Hyderabad catalyst both being real.

Scoped recommendation

If pursued

Battery packs, controllers and spare components for electric cargo three-wheelers, sold to Hyderabad fleet operators and repair shops — not the swap-network business, not cell manufacturing, not 2W/passenger EVs (weaker replacement signal, more brand-loyalty-driven). Narrow scope matches the one part of this market that's both genuinely fragmented and has proven, current-year replacement volume behind it.
