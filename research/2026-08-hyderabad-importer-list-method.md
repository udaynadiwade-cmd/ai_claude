bill-of-entry records, which are behind paid databases. Anything I claimed at
company-plus-product level without that data would be a guess dressed up as a finding.

**What I can give:** named companies from public sources, a sector-level import map, and
— most usefully — **the exact method to build the real list yourself, cheaply.**

The method section is the valuable part of this note. The company names below are a
starting scrap, not a deliverable.

## How to build the real list — three routes, cheapest first

### Route 1 — Zauba, free `start here today`

**zauba.com/shipment_search** `FACT`

Zauba carries Indian customs shipment records showing **shipment date, HS code, product,
importer name, exporter name, country, port, quantity and USD value** — pulled from
shipping manifests, bills of lading, bills of entry and shipping bills across 10,000+
ports. **Basic search is free.**

**How to use it for exactly our purpose:**
1. Search by **HS code** for the component types we care about
2. Filter for shipments arriving at Telangana / Hyderabad ports (ICD Sanathnagar,
   Hyderabad Air Cargo)
3. Read off **which Hyderabad companies are importing what, from whom, how often**

This gives what no market report can: **the actual buyer, the actual product, and proof
they buy it repeatedly.**

### Route 2 — Industry associations, free

- **Jeedimetla Industries Association** — jiahyd.com — a real association with a member
  base in exactly our target cluster `FACT`
- **FTCCI** (Federation of Telangana Chambers of Commerce and Industry)
- **TSIIC** (Telangana State Industrial Infrastructure Corporation) — allotment records
  for industrial plots

Association membership lists give names and contacts. They do not tell you who imports —
combine with Route 1.

### Route 3 — Paid customs databases `when the free route runs out`

| Provider | Note |
|---|---|
| **Volza** | Covers **129,901 active Indian importers**; searchable by company, product, HS code `FACT` |
| **Export Genius, Cybex Exim, SEAIR Exim** | Comparable shipment-level datasets |

Paid data is worth buying **only after** the free Zauba search proves the approach
works. Do not start here.

## What to actually do with the list

The list alone is not the win. The sequence is:

1. **Pull 50 Hyderabad importers from Zauba** in the component categories we can
   legally supply
2. **Sort by repeat frequency** — a company importing the same HS code four times a
   year is worth ten companies that imported once
3. **Cross-check against the QCO list** — drop anything needing BIS certification
4. **Visit the top 20 in person** with the one question that matters: *which parts do
   you buy from a local trader today that came from China, and what do you pay?*

**Zauba tells us who imports. Only the visit tells us what they pay a middleman —
which is where our margin comes from.**
