The singular customer, defined precisely

A water-treatment dealer/installer in Hyderabad — a business whose trade is selling, installing and servicing softeners, purifiers and filtration systems for homes, apartments and small commercial premises. Not a general plumber, not a general hardware store, not an industrial ETP/STP contractor — that's a different buyer, different scale, different sales cycle, and mixing them breaks the "keep the customer singular" discipline. This is also very likely D'Cal's own existing channel already, which is the whole point — the catalogue widens, the buyer doesn't change.

How to actually confirm the Hyderabad gap

  1. Check each named manufacturer's own "dealer locator" or "find a distributor" page for a Hyderabad/Telangana listing
  2. Search "[brand name] Hyderabad dealer" or "[brand name] Telangana distributor" directly
  3. Call their sales line and ask who their Hyderabad-area distributor is — the fastest, most reliable signal
  4. Cross-check against Justdial/IndiaMART Hyderabad listings for that brand specifically
