# Deadstock / excess inventory marketplaces — who is actually doing this

**Date:** August 2026 · **Status:** company research, fresh search
**Question:** who runs deadstock marketplaces, what are their numbers, do they make money?

---

## Read this first — the data in this sector is bad

**B-Stock's reported annual revenue, from three sources, on the same company:**

| Source | Figure |
|---|---|
| Latka | **$26M ARR** |
| ZoomInfo | **$150.1M** |
| LeadIQ | **$730K** |

That is a **200x spread**. Its funding history conflicts too — one source says
"bootstrapped, never raised outside money," another says **$73.2M over 3 rounds** from
Spectrum Equity and Susquehanna Growth Equity. `CLAIM — irreconcilable`

**Not one company in this sector discloses profit.** Every number below is revenue,
GMV or funding. That absence is itself a finding: if any of them were profitable, it
would be in a press release.

Treat everything here as directionally useful, not as verified accounting.

## India — the one that matters

### Excess2Sell

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | B2B marketplace for excess inventory across computer hardware, telecom, security, home appliances, apparel, lighting, kitchen goods `FACT` |
| **Lifetime GMV** | **₹1,280+ crore** `CLAIM — company-stated` |
| **Buyer network** | **60,000+ verified B2B buyers**, with deep Tier-2 and Tier-3 penetration `CLAIM — company-stated` |
| **Funding** | **~$1M**, raised just before the March 2020 lockdown `FACT` |
| **New product** | **Liquidation-as-a-Service (LaaS)** — recently launched |
| **Market claim** | India's surplus inventory opportunity is **$30 billion** `CLAIM — company-stated, unverified` |
| **Profit** | Not disclosed |

**Two things worth noticing:**

1. **₹1,280 Cr of GMV on ~$1M of funding is unusually capital-efficient** — if the GMV
   number is real. It suggests the model does not need heavy capital, which supports
   the asset-light case. It could also mean GMV is being counted generously.
2. **The company "resumed operations" at some point** — there is a press item
   specifically announcing this. `FACT` A company that had to announce resuming
   operations had stopped. Nobody says why. **This is the single most important thing
   to dig into before treating Excess2Sell as a healthy benchmark.**

### Others in India
- **stockhatao.com** — dead-stock marketplace, appears small
- **Surpluss** — tracked as an Excess2Sell competitor
- **Refash** — 130 brands, ~20% annual sales growth, but reads **consumer-facing**,
  selling finished deadstock garments rather than running a B2B trade

## United States

### Ghost — the best-funded and most interesting mechanism

| | |
|---|---|
| **Total raised** | **$95M over 6 rounds** `FACT` |
| **Series B** | $30M, 2023, led by **Cathay Innovation** `FACT` |
| **Series C** | **$40M, October 2024** `FACT` |
| **Scale** | **4,500+ brands**, 6,000+ participants, **$2B+ in listed MSRP** `CLAIM — company-stated` |
| **Growth** | Uploaded inventory **+500%** in a year; **GMV run-rate grew 10x** in the same period; headcount doubled `CLAIM — company-stated` |
| **Revenue model** | Transaction fee on each trade |
| **Profit** | Not disclosed |

**The actual innovation is secrecy, not the marketplace.** Brands will not dump stock
publicly — it damages the brand and undercuts full-price channels. Ghost's answer:
the seller stays hidden from competitors and consumers, and **the seller chooses which
buyers are allowed to see the listing.** `FACT` It also offers immediate payment to
credit-worthy sellers.

That is the insight worth stealing: **in deadstock, discretion is the product.** The
marketplace is just the delivery mechanism.

### B-Stock
- Online **auction** platform for liquidation and overstock
- Described as one of the largest B2B liquidation networks
- **Direct-sourcing model that cuts out intermediary liquidator markups** `FACT`
- Employees: 254 (one source) to 500–1,000 (another) — again, conflicting
- Revenue and funding: see the warning at the top of this note

### Forthclear
- New entrant, 2026. **Free to list, 5% of item subtotal charged to the seller on
  sale** `FACT`
- Founder selected for Replit's inaugural Race to Revenue cohort
- Small — worth watching for pricing model, not for scale

### BlueLots
- Connects Amazon, Shopify and eBay resellers to bulk liquidation suppliers
- **Live auctions plus fixed Buy Now listings**

## Market size

- **Global excess inventory liquidation, apparel only:** $22.4B (2025) → $41.2B
  (2034), 7.8% CAGR `FACT`
- **India excess inventory market:** projected **15–20% CAGR over five years** `FACT`
- **India surplus inventory opportunity:** $30B `CLAIM — Excess2Sell's own figure, not
  independently verified`

India is growing roughly twice as fast as the global market, which is the strongest
argument in favour of the category.

## What this means for Befach — the honest verdict

**The category is real, growing, and already has an incumbent in India.**

Excess2Sell has ₹1,280 Cr of lifetime GMV and 60,000 buyers. That is not an empty
market. It is the same pattern found for bearings (Ranigunj), electricals (Begum
Bazaar), water (Romegamart) and medical supplies (Medikabazaar) — **a market assumed
open that has someone in it already.**

**The difference here, and it is worth stating fairly:** Excess2Sell raised only ~$1M
and appears to have stopped and restarted once. That is a materially weaker incumbent
than Medikabazaar ($185M raised, ₹1,600 Cr revenue) or Romegamart. A weak incumbent in
a fast-growing category is a different situation from a dominant one.

**But the objection from the earlier scan stands unchanged:** deadstock uses none of
Befach's import machinery, none of the API access, and none of the two years of
customs experience. It is a domestic marketplace business that anyone could start.

**Before considering it seriously, one question needs an answer:** why did Excess2Sell
stop operating, and what changed when it resumed? If the answer is "the model does not
generate enough margin to survive," that is decisive. If the answer is "COVID," it is
not.
