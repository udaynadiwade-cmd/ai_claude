Research note · Alibaba taxonomy screened fresh, Hyderabad-first · August 2026

Thirty-six doors

Every one of Alibaba's 36 top-level categories, screened from scratch against five criteria the founder set directly: high revenue, good margins, velocity of goods movement, easy business model, easy receipt of payments — plus whether Hyderabad gives a real edge. No prior framework applied first; this restarts from the category list and these five criteria, per the standing rule against defaulting to a lens before looking.

Eliminated on sight — 22 of 36

Fails velocity — capital equipment, bought once and used for years (8)

The proven filter from earlier in this series, applied fresh: Commercial Equipment & Machinery, Construction & Building Machinery, Industrial Machinery (the machines themselves — parts/consumables inside this bucket are treated separately below), Home Appliances, Furniture, Testing Instrument & Equipment, Material Handling, Vehicles & Transportation (vehicles themselves — parts are separate). Financed or project-purchased, not reordered — fails velocity by definition regardless of revenue or margin.

No Hyderabad edge, and already crowded nationally (14)

Home & Garden, Sports & Entertainment, Sportswear & Outdoor Apparel, Apparel & Accessories, Shoes & Accessories, Luggage/Bags & Cases, Parents/Kids & Toys, Gifts & Crafts, Pet Supplies, School & Office Supplies, Lights & Lighting, Packaging & Printing (Bizongo-owned already), Personal Care & Home Care and Beauty (brand-dominated FMCG, brutal margins for a new entrant), Consumer Electronics (BIS/QCO compliance minefield, already found in an earlier note in this series), Renewable Energy (early, complex, no Hyderabad specificity), Safety & Security, Vehicle Parts & Accessories and Automotive Supplies & Tools (fitment-complexity risk already flagged, no unique Hyderabad cluster), Electronic Components (Bangalore/Chennai own this nationally), Agriculture/Food & Beverage (Telangana agri is real but not globally distinctive), Construction & Real Estate (a services/project category, not a goods trade).

The halo to watch for

Hyderabad's pharma dominance is real and famous enough that it's tempting to call it the obvious answer. It fails the two criteria the founder weighted explicitly — easy business model and easy receipt of payments — by more than either other candidate. A big, famous local cluster is not the same thing as an easy first move; naming that plainly rather than letting cluster prestige stand in for a criteria match.

Recommendation

Top pick

Power Transmission / Tools & Hardware — bearings, fasteners, welding consumables — sold to Hyderabad-based factories and workshops. Wins on the combination of all five stated criteria, not just one: validated revenue, real (non-brand-driven) margin, proven reorder velocity, the only candidate that clears "easy business model" cleanly, and cash-based payment at the point of sale. Hyderabad is the demand side here, not a production cluster — a large industrial city with genuine local factory buyers — which still satisfies "start in Hyderabad," just via a different mechanism than a local manufacturing cluster.

Second: Jewelry/pearls — real local cluster, but loses on velocity and on an unresolved revenue number. Third: Pharma raw materials — the biggest, most famous Hyderabad cluster of the three, and the worst fit on the founder's own "easy" criteria.
