# NSE Industry Classification Structure — full reference

**Status:** Founder-supplied, official NSE Indices classification (12 Macro-Economic
Sectors → 22 Sectors → 59 Industries → 197 Basic Industries). This is the
**India-specific counterpart to GICS** — GICS is global/MSCI-S&P, this is NSE's own
structure used for Indian listed-company classification and NIFTY sectoral indices.

**What's verified vs not, plainly:** the 4-tier structure below (MES → Sector →
Industry → Basic Industry, all codes IN01–IN12) is the official NSE structure as
supplied — treat as `[FACT]`. The accompanying "expanded list" commentary, NIFTY 500
company counts/weights, and especially the **CAGR growth-rate figures** (EV 20–28%,
Renewable Energy 18–25%, AI 20–25%, etc.) that came with it were **not independently
verified this session** — they read as AI-generated summary content from wherever the
founder sourced this, not sourced to a named report. Treat those figures as
`[CLAIM]`, not `[FACT]`, until checked against a primary source.

## Full 4-tier structure

### IN01 — Commodities
**IN0101 Chemicals**
- IN010101 Chemicals & Petrochemicals — Commodity Chemicals, Specialty Chemicals, Carbon Black, Dyes & Pigments, Explosives, Petrochemicals, Printing Inks, Trading-Chemicals, Industrial Gases
- IN010102 Fertilizers & Agrochemicals — Fertilizers, Pesticides & Agrochemicals

**IN0102 Construction Materials**
- IN010203 Cement & Cement Products
- IN010204 Other Construction Materials (sand etc., excludes granites/marbles)

**IN0103 Metals & Mining**
- IN010301 Ferrous Metals — Ferro & Silica Manganese, Pig Iron, Sponge Iron, Iron & Steel
- IN010302 Non-Ferrous Metals — Aluminum, Copper, Zinc, Precious Metals
- IN010303 Diversified Metals
- IN010304 Minerals & Mining — Industrial Minerals (excludes coal)
- IN010305 Metals & Minerals Trading — Trading-Metals, Trading-Minerals

**IN0104 Forest Materials**
- IN010401 Paper, Forest & Jute Products — Paper & Paper Products, Forest Products, Jute & Jute Products

### IN02 — Consumer Discretionary
**IN0201 Automobile and Auto Components**
- IN020101 Automobiles — Passenger Cars & UVs, 2/3 Wheelers, Trading-Automobiles
- IN020102 Auto Components — Auto Components & Equipments, **Batteries-Automobile**, **Fastener**, Gas Cylinders, Trading-Auto Ancillaries, Tyres & Rubber Products

**IN0202 Consumer Durables** — Air Conditioner, Cycles, Consumer Electronics, Furniture/Home Furnishing/Flooring, Gems Jewellery & Watches, Glass-Consumer, Household Appliances, Houseware, Leather Products, Leisure Products, Plastic Products-Consumer, Plywood/Laminates, Sanitary Ware, Toys, Paints, Diversified Consumer Products

**IN0203 Textiles** — Garments & Apparels, Other Textile Products, Trading-Textile Products

**IN0204 Media, Entertainment & Publication** — Media (Advertising/Electronic/Web/Print), Entertainment (Film/Digital/TV), Printing & Publication

**IN0205 Realty** — Residential/Commercial Projects, Real Estate Services, REITs

**IN0206 Consumer Services**
- IN020601 Leisure Services — Hotels & Resorts, Restaurants, Amusement Parks, Wellness, Tour/Travel
- IN020602 Other Consumer Services — Education, E-Learning, Food Storage Facilities, Other Consumer Services
- IN020603 Retailing — Speciality Retail, Pharmacy Retail, Diversified Retail, E-Retail, Internet & Catalogue Retail, **Distributors** (wholesalers of general merchandise)

### IN03 — Energy
**IN0301 Oil, Gas & Consumable Fuels** — Gas (Transmission/Industrial/LPG-CNG-PNG-LNG/Trading), Oil (E&P, Offshore, Storage & Transport, Equipment & Services), Petroleum Products (Refineries & Marketing, Lubricants), Consumable Fuels (Coal)

### IN04 — Fast Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)
**IN0401** — Agricultural Food & Other Products (Edible Oil, Sugar, Tea & Coffee), Beverages (Breweries/Distilleries, Other Beverages), Cigarettes & Tobacco, Food Products (Animal Feed, Dairy, Other Food, Packaged Foods), **Personal Products** (toothpaste, shampoo, perfume, cosmetics), Household Products (aluminum foils, detergents, matchsticks, paper tissues, soaps, **Batteries-Home appliance**, Photographic Products, Stationery), Diversified FMCG

### IN05 — Financial Services
**IN0501** — Finance (Financial Institution, Housing Finance, Investment Company, NBFC, Other Financial Services, Holding Company), Banks (Public/Private/Other), Capital Markets (AMC, Depositories, Financial Product Distributor, Ratings, Exchange & Data Platform, Stockbroking), Insurance (General/Life/Other/Distributors), **Financial Technology (Fintech)**

### IN06 — Healthcare
**IN0601** — Pharmaceuticals & Biotechnology, Healthcare Equipment & Supplies (**Medical Equipment & Supplies** — BP monitors, X-Ray, MRI, syringes, gloves), Healthcare Services (Hospital, Healthcare Service Provider, Healthcare Research/Analytics/Tech)

### IN07 — Industrials
**IN0701 Construction** — Civil Construction, Engineering/Designing & Construction (EPC)

**IN0702 Capital Goods**
- IN070201 Aerospace & Defense
- IN070202 Agricultural/Commercial/Construction Vehicles — Tractors, Commercial Vehicles, Construction Vehicles
- IN070203 **Electrical Equipment** — Heavy Electrical Equipment, **Other Electrical Equipment** (electric cables, wires, other electrical components) ← *electricals pick lives here*
- IN070204 Industrial Manufacturing — Elevators, Engineering & Construction products, Industrial Electronics, Industrial Equipments, Railway Wagons, Ship Building, **Industrial Machinery** ← *welding/machinery consumables live here*
- IN070205 **Industrial Products** — Abrasives, **Bearings (excludes auto bearings)** ← *bearings pick lives here*, Cables-Electricals, Castings & Forgings, Compressors & Pumps, Diesel Engines, Electrodes, **Packaging** (cardboard, bags, boxes, cans, drums, bottles, jars, glass), Plastic Products-Industrial, Refractories, Rubber, Other Industrial Products, Glass-Industrial, Aluminium/Copper/Zinc Products, Iron & Steel Products

### IN08 — Information Technology
**IN0801** — IT-Software (Computers-Software & Consulting, Software Products), IT-Services (IT Enabled Services), IT-Hardware (Computers Hardware & Equipment)

### IN09 — Services
**IN0901** — Engineering Services (Dredging), Transport Services (Airline, Logistics Solution Provider, Railways, Road Transport, Shipping), Transport Infrastructure (Airport, Port, Toll bridge), **Commercial Services & Supplies** (**Trading & Distributors**, Consulting Services, Data Processing Services, Diversified Commercial Services, BPO/KPO)

### IN10 — Telecommunication
**IN1001** — Telecom-Services (Cellular/Fixed line, Infrastructure, Other), Telecom-Equipment & Accessories

### IN11 — Utilities
**IN1101 Power** — Electric Utilities, Power Trading, Power-Transmission
**IN1102 Utilities** — **Water Supply & Management**, Waste Management, Emergency Services, Multi Utilities, Other Utilities

### IN12 — Diversified
**IN1201** — Diversified (no single segment >50% revenue)
