## About the role

This is a full-stack digital marketing role, and it exists for one reason: to
put qualified leads in front of our sales team at a cost we can defend.

You will own the whole funnel — the ads that create demand, the search presence
that captures it, the content and social that build trust in between, and the
email and CRM flows that keep a lead warm until sales can close it. You will
also own the number that matters: cost per qualified lead.

This is an execution role, not a strategy role. You will be in Ads Manager, in
GA4, in the sheet, most days. But you will not be handed a plan to follow — you
will build it, run it, measure it, and tell us what to kill.

## What you will do

**Paid performance — the core of the job**

- Plan, launch and manage campaigns on **Google Ads** (Search, Performance Max,
  Display) and **Meta Ads Manager** (Facebook, Instagram).
- Own budget pacing, bidding, audience and placement decisions day to day.
- Build and test landing pages and lead forms; run structured A/B tests on
  creative, hook and offer.
- Report on **CPL, cost per qualified lead, CTR, ROAS and lead-to-sale rate** —
  not impressions and likes.

**SEO and content**

- On-page and technical SEO: titles, meta, schema, internal linking, page speed,
  indexation.
- Keyword research and a content calendar that targets real commercial intent.
- Off-page: backlinks, local SEO, and Google Business Profile.
- Write or brief blog posts, landing page copy and ad copy.

**Organic social and creative**

- Run the content calendar across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn.
- Produce short-form video and static creative — Canva or Figma level is fine,
  you do not need to be a designer, you need to be able to ship.
- Manage community: comments, DMs, and routing genuine enquiries to sales same-day.

**Email, CRM and automation**

- Build lead-nurture and lifecycle email or WhatsApp sequences.
- Keep lists clean and segmented; measure open, click and conversion.
- Make sure every lead from every channel lands in the CRM with its source
  attached. Attribution is part of the job, not an afterthought.

**Measurement across all of it**

- Own **GA4**, Google Tag Manager, Search Console, and the Meta pixel /
  Conversions API. Make sure conversion tracking is actually firing correctly.
- Publish a weekly one-page dashboard: spend, leads, CPL, quality, what changed
  and what you are changing next.

## What we are looking for

**Must have**

- 1–3 years running digital marketing, with hands-on ownership of a live ad
  budget. Not shadowing, not reporting on someone else's account.
- Working command of **Google Ads and Meta Ads Manager**, and **GA4**.
- The ability to read a performance report and say what to do next. We will ask
  you to interpret a real dashboard in the interview.
- Solid written English — you will be writing ad copy and landing pages.
- Genuine comfort with spreadsheets.

**Strongly preferred**

- SEO experience with a tool like Semrush, Ahrefs or Search Console at depth.
- Google Tag Manager and conversion tracking setup, including server-side or
  Meta CAPI.
- Experience marketing a **high-ticket considered purchase** — property, loans,
  education, healthcare — where the lead is qualified by a human afterwards.
- Email or WhatsApp automation via Mailchimp, HubSpot, Zoho or similar.
- Short-form video editing.
- Google Ads or Meta Blueprint certification.

**Realistic expectation from us**

We know that one person who is genuinely strong at all four pillars at 1–3 years
is rare. If you are excellent at paid performance and measurement and competent
at the rest, apply. We would rather hire depth in the channel that drives revenue
and build the rest around you.
