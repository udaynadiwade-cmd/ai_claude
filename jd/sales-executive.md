## About the role

Befach makes D'Cal — a low-cost, no-maintenance hard water softener. Nearly
every home in Hyderabad has a hard water problem; D'Cal solves it for a
fraction of what a traditional softener costs. Your job is to get that in
front of the people who need it and close the sale.

Customers are ordinary consumers — not businesses, not procurement teams.
You'll talk to people in whatever language they're comfortable in, explain a
simple product simply, and close.

## What you will actually do

- Call, message, and meet consumer leads every day — inbound enquiries and
  leads you generate yourself.
- Explain the product in Hindi, English, Telugu, or whatever language the
  customer speaks. Switch naturally.
- Handle price pushback, "let me think about it," and silence — without
  dropping the deal.
- Close the sale and get to a decision, mostly within a few touches.
- Log every conversation with a dated next step.
- Hit a monthly number and report it yourself, without being chased.
- Work from the Banjara Hills office — this is not a remote or field-only role.

## What we're looking for

**This is 80% of the decision:**

- **Very high attitude.** Founder mentality — you take the problem as yours,
  you don't wait to be told the next step.
- **You've done things.** A project, a side hustle, freelance work, anything
  self-started. We care that you tried, and what you did next when it didn't work.
- **You genuinely like talking to people.** This job is conversation, all day,
  often with strangers.
- **Solutions, not problems.** When something's in the way, you come with two
  ways round it.
- **You can think.** You can reason through a situation you haven't seen before.
- **Hardworking.** This is not a coast-through job.

**Practical:**

- **Multilingual — Hindi, English, and Telugu required.** Other regional
  languages are a plus.
- Able to work full-time from our Banjara Hills, Hyderabad office.
- Comfortable with a phone, a spreadsheet, and following a process.

**Not required:** a specific degree, a specific college, or prior sales
experience. If you've sold anything — including your own idea to someone —
tell us about it.

## What we offer
