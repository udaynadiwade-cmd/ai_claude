```
ABOUT THE ROLE

We sell directly to consumers, and every sale is a considered decision someone makes with real money. That means the person on our side of the conversation matters more than the brochure.

We are looking for a Sales Executive who can pick up a cold lead, build trust in the first ninety seconds, handle the objections that come with a high-ticket purchase, and close.

If you have sold loans, plots, property or insurance, you already know this job. You know what it means to work a pipeline that does not close on the first call. You know the difference between a lead that is warm and a lead that is just polite.

We are not looking for someone who processes enquiries. We are looking for someone who chases them.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Own a pipeline of inbound and self-generated leads end to end — first call to closed sale.
2. Make a high volume of outbound calls daily and convert conversations into scheduled meetings or site visits.
3. Qualify hard and early — understand budget, intent and timeline before investing a week in someone.
4. Handle price, trust and "let me think about it" objections without dropping the deal or dropping the price.
5. Follow up relentlessly and systematically. Most of our business closes on the 4th to 7th touch.
6. Keep the CRM clean and current — every call logged, every next step dated.
7. Hit and beat a monthly sales target, and report your own numbers weekly.
8. Share field intelligence with the marketing team — what customers object to, which lead sources convert.

CANDIDATE REQUIREMENTS

Must have:
- 1-3 years in a direct, target-carrying B2C sales role. You have personally closed deals.
- Outstanding spoken communication in English and [REGIONAL LANGUAGE].
- A genuine liking for talking to people — this is conversation all day, often with strangers, often with people who say no.
- A closer's temperament. Comfortable asking for the order. Comfortable hearing no.
- Your actual numbers: target vs achieved, deal sizes, conversion rate.
- Comfort with a CRM, or disciplined self-tracking at minimum.

Strongly preferred:
- Experience selling home loans, personal loans, LAP, real estate plots, residential or commercial property, or insurance.
- Experience working leads generated by digital ad campaigns.
- An existing local network in [CITY].

Not required: any specific degree. We care what you have sold and how much of it.

WHAT WE OFFER

- Fixed [₹X.X - ₹X.X LPA] plus UNCAPPED incentive: [STATE STRUCTURE].
  Our top performers should out-earn their fixed salary.
- A steady flow of qualified leads from a dedicated digital marketing function.
  You sell — you do not spend your day hunting for phone numbers.
- Direct access to the decision maker. Short chain of command, fast approvals.
- [Travel allowance / insurance / other benefits]

HOW TO APPLY

Complete the form: [GOOGLE FORM LINK]
Attach your CV and tell us in 2-3 lines: what you sold, your monthly target, and what you actually achieved against it. Honest answers get read first.

We review applications twice a week and reply to every applicant either way.
```

### Job description body

```
TO APPLY: Please complete this 5-minute form — [GOOGLE FORM LINK]

ABOUT THE ROLE

This is a full-stack digital marketing role, and it exists for one reason: to put qualified leads in front of our sales team at a cost we can defend.

You will own the whole funnel — the ads that create demand, the search presence that captures it, the content and social that build trust in between, and the email and CRM flows that keep a lead warm until sales can close it. You will also own the number that matters: cost per qualified lead.

This is an execution role, not a strategy role. You will be in Ads Manager, in GA4, in the sheet, most days. But you will not be handed a plan to follow — you will build it, run it, measure it, and tell us what to kill.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Paid performance (the core of the job):
- Plan, launch and manage Google Ads (Search, Performance Max, Display) and Meta Ads (Facebook, Instagram).
- Own budget pacing, bidding, audience and placement decisions day to day.
- Build and test landing pages and lead forms; run structured A/B tests on creative, hook and offer.
- Report on CPL, cost per qualified lead, CTR, ROAS and lead-to-sale rate.

SEO and content:
- On-page and technical SEO — titles, meta, schema, internal linking, page speed, indexation.
- Keyword research and a content calendar targeting commercial intent.
- Off-page, local SEO and Google Business Profile.
- Write or brief blog posts, landing page copy and ad copy.

Organic social and creative:
- Run the content calendar across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and LinkedIn.
- Produce short-form video and static creative (Canva or Figma level is fine).
- Manage comments and DMs; route genuine enquiries to sales same-day.

Email, CRM and automation:
- Build lead-nurture sequences over email and WhatsApp.
- Keep lists clean and segmented; measure open, click and conversion.
- Ensure every lead lands in the CRM with its source attached.

Measurement:
- Own GA4, Google Tag Manager, Search Console and the Meta pixel / Conversions API.
- Publish a weekly one-page dashboard: spend, leads, CPL, quality, what changed, what is changing next.

CANDIDATE REQUIREMENTS

Must have:
- 1-3 years hands-on, with ownership of a live ad budget. Not shadowing, not reporting on someone else's account.
- Working command of Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager and GA4.
- Ability to read a performance report and say what to do next. We will ask you to interpret a real dashboard in the interview.
- Solid written English — you will write ad copy and landing pages.
- Genuine comfort with spreadsheets.

Strongly preferred:
- SEO depth with Semrush, Ahrefs or Search Console.
- Google Tag Manager and conversion tracking setup, including server-side or Meta CAPI.
- Experience marketing a high-ticket considered purchase — property, loans, education, healthcare — where a human qualifies the lead afterwards.
- Email or WhatsApp automation (Mailchimp, HubSpot, Zoho).
- Short-form video editing.
- Google Ads or Meta Blueprint certification.

A realistic note: we know one person genuinely strong at all four pillars at 1-3 years is rare. If you are excellent at paid performance and measurement and competent at the rest, please apply.

WHAT WE OFFER

- [₹X.X - ₹X.X LPA] fixed.
- Full ownership of a [₹X]/month ad budget from month one — real spend, real decisions, real accountability.
- A sales team that closes what you generate, so you get closed-loop feedback on lead quality instead of guessing.
- Budget for tools and certifications.

HOW TO APPLY

[GOOGLE FORM LINK] — attach your CV and describe one campaign you ran: the channel, the monthly budget, the cost per lead when you started, and the cost per lead when you finished. Numbers, not adjectives. That answer decides who we call.

We review applications twice a week and reply to every applicant either way.
```

## Platform-specific adjustments

**Apna / WorkIndia** — built for frontline hiring and mobile-first candidates.
Trim the sales JD to the first 6 responsibilities and the must-have list; long
posts underperform there. Set the required-skills tags carefully, as their
matching is tag-driven. These platforms also let you set language requirements
and whether a two-wheeler is needed — fill those in.

**Indeed** — put the form link in the "Application URL" field rather than
collecting via Indeed's inbox. Free to post; sponsor only if week-one volume is
thin.

**Naukri** — the "Key Skills" field drives their matching engine harder than the
body text. Fill all 15+ keywords. If you buy Resdex access, tell me and I will
give you the exact boolean search strings to pull candidates directly rather than
waiting for applications.

**Foundit / Shine** — same copy, no changes needed. Lower volume; treat as
supplementary.
