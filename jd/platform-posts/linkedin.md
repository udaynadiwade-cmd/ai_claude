# LinkedIn — ready to paste

Two formats per role: the **job posting** (structured fields) and the
**personal feed post**. The personal post reliably out-performs the job posting
for roles at this level — do both.

---

## B. Job posting — Digital Marketing Executive

**Job title:** `Digital Marketing Executive`
**Seniority:** Associate · **Function:** Marketing
**Skills:** Google Ads, Meta Ads, Performance Marketing, SEO, Google Analytics
(GA4), Google Tag Manager, SEM, Email Marketing, Social Media Marketing, Content
Marketing
**Apply:** External website → `[GOOGLE FORM LINK]`

**Description:**

```
This role exists for one reason: to put qualified leads in front of our sales team at a cost we can defend.

You'll own the whole funnel — the ads that create demand, the search presence that captures it, the content and social that build trust in between, and the email and CRM flows that keep a lead warm until sales can close it. And you'll own the number that matters: cost per qualified lead.

This is an execution role. You'll be in Ads Manager, in GA4, in the sheet, most days. But you won't be handed a plan — you'll build it, run it, measure it, and tell us what to kill.

WHAT YOU'LL DO
• Paid: Google Ads (Search, PMax) and Meta Ads — budget pacing, bidding, audiences, creative testing, landing pages
• SEO: on-page, technical, keyword research, off-page, local SEO and Google Business Profile
• Social & creative: content calendar across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn; short-form video; Canva/Figma
• Email & CRM: nurture sequences, segmentation, lead source attribution
• Measurement: own GA4, GTM, Search Console, Meta pixel/CAPI. Publish a weekly one-page dashboard

MUST HAVE
• 1–3 years hands-on, with ownership of a live ad budget — not shadowing, not reporting on someone else's account
• Working command of Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager and GA4
• Ability to read a performance report and say what to do next (we'll test this)
• Solid written English — you'll write the ad copy

STRONGLY PREFERRED
SEO at depth (Semrush/Ahrefs/Search Console) · GTM and conversion tracking setup · experience marketing a high-ticket product where a salesperson closes the lead · email automation · short-form video · Google Ads or Meta Blueprint certification

BEING REALISTIC
One person genuinely strong at all four pillars at 1–3 years is rare. If you're excellent at paid performance and measurement and competent at the rest — apply.

WHAT WE OFFER
• [₹X.X–₹X.X LPA]
• Full ownership of a [₹X]/month ad budget from month one
• A sales team that closes what you generate, so you get closed-loop feedback on lead quality instead of guessing
• Budget for tools and certifications

HOW TO APPLY
[GOOGLE FORM LINK] — attach your CV and tell us about one campaign: channel, monthly budget, starting CPL, ending CPL. Numbers, not adjectives.
```
