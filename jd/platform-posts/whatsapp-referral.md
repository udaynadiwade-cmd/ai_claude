# WhatsApp, Instagram and referral copy

Referrals convert better than any job board, at zero cost. Send these on day one,
before you spend a rupee on a portal.

---

## WhatsApp — broadcast to your network

**Sales Executive**

```
Hi 👋 We're hiring a *Sales Executive* at [COMPANY] in [CITY].

Looking for someone with 1–3 years selling *loans, plots, property or insurance* — someone who genuinely enjoys talking to people and knows how to close.

• On-site in [CITY]
• [₹X.X–₹X.X LPA] fixed + *uncapped* incentive
• Qualified leads provided — no cold list hunting

Apply here (4 min): [FORM LINK]

If someone comes to mind, please pass this on 🙏
```

**Digital Marketing Executive**

```
Hi 👋 We're hiring a *Digital Marketing Executive* at [COMPANY] in [CITY].

1–3 years, hands-on with *Google Ads + Meta Ads + GA4*. SEO, social and email experience is a bonus. You'd own a real ad budget from month one.

• On-site in [CITY]
• [₹X.X–₹X.X LPA]

Apply here (5 min): [FORM LINK]

Please share with anyone who fits 🙏
```

**Research Manager**

```
Hi 👋 We're hiring a *Research Manager* at [COMPANY] in [CITY] — market and consumer research.

4–7 years. Someone who designs the study *and* runs the fieldwork: market sizing, competitor and pricing work, buyer interviews, lost-deal research.

• On-site in [CITY]
• [₹X.X–₹X.X LPA]
• Findings go straight to the founder and get acted on — no layer in between

Most likely fit: someone at a research or advisory firm (Kantar, Nielsen, Ipsos, Anarock, Knight Frank, Liases Foras type) who's tired of never finding out what happened to their work.

Apply here (6 min): [FORM LINK]

If someone comes to mind, please pass this on 🙏
```

> **Naming the target firms is deliberate.** Your contacts do not know what a
> research manager is, but they do know whether they know someone at Kantar. A
> referral ask this specific gets roughly double the hit rate of a generic one.

## Instagram / Facebook — story and feed post

> **Sales and DME only.** Do not post the Research Manager role here. That pool
> is not browsing Instagram for jobs, and mixing a 4–7 year specialist role into
> a frontline hiring post makes both look less serious.

**Story (design in Canva, 1080×1920):**

> **WE'RE HIRING**
> Sales Executive
> Digital Marketing Executive
> 📍 `[CITY]` · 1–3 years
> *Swipe up / link in bio to apply*

**Feed caption:**

```
We're hiring two people in [CITY]. 📍

1️⃣ SALES EXECUTIVE — 1–3 yrs. If you've sold loans, plots, property or insurance, you already know this job. Fixed salary + uncapped incentive, and a marketing team that hands you qualified leads.

2️⃣ DIGITAL MARKETING EXECUTIVE — 1–3 yrs. Google Ads, Meta, SEO, GA4. You'd own a real budget from month one and get closed-loop feedback from a sales team that actually closes your leads.

Both on-site. Both open now.

Apply → link in bio.
Tag someone 👇

#hiring #[CITY]jobs #saleshiring #digitalmarketingjobs #jobsin[CITY] #nowhiring
```

> For the DME role specifically, an Instagram post is not just outreach — it is a
> filter. Candidates who find you through your own social and reference it in
> the application have demonstrated exactly the channel awareness you are
> hiring for. Ask me to flag those.

## Direct outreach — the highest-yield channel nobody uses

For the sales role, the best candidates are employed and not browsing job
boards. They are sitting in the loan department of a local bank or NBFC, or on
the floor of a real estate office in `[CITY]`.

For the **Research Manager** this is not a bonus channel, it is *the* channel —
the target firm list and search filters are in `pipeline/POSTING-PLAYBOOK.md`.

Ask me to write you a short LinkedIn connection-request script and a follow-up
message for approaching them directly. Twenty targeted messages typically
out-performs a week of a paid job posting for either profile.
