# WhatsApp, Instagram and referral copy

Referrals convert better than any job board, at zero cost. Send these on day one,
before you spend a rupee on a portal.

> For the DME role specifically, an Instagram post is not just outreach — it is a
> filter. Candidates who find you through your own social and reference it in
> the application have demonstrated exactly the channel awareness you are
> hiring for. Ask me to flag those.

## Direct outreach — the highest-yield channel nobody uses

For the sales role, the best candidates are employed and not browsing job
boards. They are sitting in the loan department of a local bank or NBFC, or on
the floor of a real estate office in `[CITY]`.

Ask me to write you a short LinkedIn connection-request script and a follow-up
message for approaching them directly. Twenty targeted messages typically
out-performs a week of a paid job posting for this profile.
