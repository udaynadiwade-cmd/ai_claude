**Location:** Banjara Hills, Hyderabad · On-site
**Experience:** 1–2 years
**Fixed:** ₹3.6–4 lakhs per annum (₹30,000–33,300/month)
**Reports to:** Founder
**Works with:** Alongside Vamshi on quick-commerce; coordinates our marketplace

---

## About the role

Befach sells two consumer products that belong on every major Indian
marketplace: **D'Cal water softeners** and **Befach Rice** (low-GI,
diabetic-friendly).

Today those products are sold largely through distributors, dealers and offline
channels. This role exists to build the marketplace channel properly — to own
Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and the rest end to end, and to make them a real
revenue line rather than a listing that happens to exist.

You are not joining a team that already does this well. You are the person who
makes it work.

---

## What you will actually do

**List and maintain**
- Create and optimise listings across Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, and other
  marketplaces as we add them
- Own the catalogue: titles, bullet points, descriptions, images, A+ content,
  keywords, SKUs, variants, pricing
- Keep listings live and healthy — suppressed listings, stock sync, buy-box
  issues, category and compliance problems

**Sell**
- Own the sales number on each platform, not just the listing quality
- Coordinate the external agencies that handle platform registration and
  q-commerce onboarding — brief them, hold them to timelines, check their work
- Plan around platform sale events (Great Indian Festival, Big Billion Days,
  Meesho Mega Sale) rather than reacting to them
- Work with the warehouse team on stock, dispatch SLAs and returns

**Advertise**
- Run Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, PLA and equivalent campaigns
- Manage keyword bids, negative keywords, budget allocation across platforms
- Kill what isn't working quickly and say why

**Measure**
- Calculate and report true ROI per platform, per campaign, per SKU
- Track ACOS/TACOS, contribution margin after platform commission, shipping,
  returns and ad spend
- Bring a monthly view of where the money actually came from — and where it leaked

## What we're looking for

**This is most of the decision:**

- **You have personally run marketplace accounts.** Not assisted. Not pulled
  reports on accounts someone else ran. You have logged into Seller Central or
  Flipkart Seller Hub yourself and made changes that moved a number.
- **You can quantify your own work.** "I took ACOS from 42% to 26% over two
  months on a ₹1.5L monthly spend" is what a real answer looks like. Vague
  claims of "handled e-commerce" tell us nothing.
- **You understand that a sale is not a profit.** Anyone can buy revenue with ad
  spend. We care whether the unit made money after commission, shipping,
  returns and ads.
- **Communication.** You will talk to platform account managers, our warehouse
  team, and the founders. You need to explain a number clearly and say plainly
  when something isn't working.

**Practical:**
- 1–2 years hands-on across at least one major marketplace; more than one is better
- Comfortable in Excel or Sheets — pivot tables, basic formulas, building a
  simple P&L per SKU
- Able to work on-site in Hyderabad

**Not required:** a specific degree, a specific college, an agency background, or
experience with our exact product categories.
