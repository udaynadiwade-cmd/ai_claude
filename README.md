## Division of labour — be clear on this

**I cannot log into Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed or Apna and click "Post."** Those
need an authenticated employer account, and I have no credentials for them —
which is as it should be. Everything up to and after that click is mine.

| I do | You do |
|---|---|
| Research the market and write the JDs | Give me company details and salary bands |
| Tell you where each role's candidates actually are — the boards, and for the Research Manager the named firms to approach directly | Send the twenty outreach messages (~30 min) |
| Rewrite them per platform, with the titles and keywords that drive visibility | Copy-paste and hit publish (~10 min/platform) |
| Build the form spec, rubrics, interview kits, email templates | Build the three Google Forms (~10 min each) |
| Read every application and CV, score them, rank them | Approve or override my calls |
| Draft every candidate email, personalised | Hit send |
| Track the funnel and tell you which platforms are worth the money | Interview and decide |

If you would rather the posting itself were automated, an ATS with an API —
Zoho Recruit, Keka, Freshteam — would let me push to every board directly.
Say the word and I will set that up instead.
