# Candidates

This folder is the pipeline record. `tracker.csv` is the master list; I keep it
in sync with the Drive tracker sheet.

## Stages

`Applied` → `Screened` → `Phone screen` → `Exercise/Roleplay` → `Final` →
`Offer` → `Hired` / `Rejected` / `On hold`

## How to use this with me

Say any of these and I will do the work:

| You say | I do |
|---|---|
| "Screen the new applications" | Read every new form response, download and read each CV, score against the rubric, update the tracker, give you a ranked shortlist |
| "Show me the shortlist for sales" | Ranked list with score, evidence, and my recommended action per candidate |
| "Why did you reject #SE-0014?" | The full scoring breakdown and the exact lines from their application that drove it |
| "I disagree, move them to interview" | Override applied, tracker updated, and I adjust how I weight that signal going forward |
| "Draft the phone screen invites for the top 5" | Five personalised drafts referencing something specific from each application, ready for you to send |
| "How is the funnel doing?" | Applications by source, conversion at each stage, cost per qualified applicant, time-to-hire, and where the pipeline is leaking |
| "Set up interviews with these 3" | Calendar invites drafted against your availability |

## Rules I follow

1. **I never auto-reject.** Every reject is a recommendation until you confirm.
2. **I never email a candidate without your approval.** You see every draft.
3. **I always show evidence.** Every score cites the specific line from the
   application or CV that produced it.
4. **I flag rather than filter on anything sensitive.** Employment gaps, career
   changes, unfamiliar employers and non-linear paths get surfaced as questions
   for interview, never scored down silently.
5. **I tell you when I am uncertain.** A CV that could be read two ways gets
   flagged as such rather than given a confident wrong score.

## Data note

Candidate CVs contain personal data. They live in your Drive, not in this git
repo — `.gitignore` blocks resume files from ever being committed. `tracker.csv`
is designed to hold a working record; if you would rather it held no names or
phone numbers at all, say so and I will keep those fields in the Drive sheet only.
