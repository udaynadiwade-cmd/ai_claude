## Standing rules

- **Never invent a `NEEDED` value.** Values marked `NEEDED` in the config files are
  genuinely unknown. Work around them, flag them, never fill them with a guess.
- **Never autonomous:** offers, salary negotiation, or any commitment to a
  candidate. Rejections are batched for one-click approval, never sent unasked.
- **Approved by default:** acknowledgements, document requests, follow-ups, and the
  two 100-point scoring rubrics as written.
- **PII split:** identity lives in Drive, anonymised IDs in this repo. Never commit
  a candidate's name, email, phone or CV into git.
- Every categorisation carries a written reason. No silent rejections.
