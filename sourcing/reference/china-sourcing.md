# China Sourcing — Vetting, Clusters and Calendar

## Is it a factory or a trading company?

Run every candidate through this before sending a sample order:

1. **Pull the corporate record** on SAMR (`gsxt.gov.cn`), cross-checked on Tianyancha (天眼查) or
   Qichacha (企查查). Verify:
   - Registered Chinese name matches the name on the proforma invoice and the bank account.
   - **Business scope (经营范围)** includes manufacturing of your product category, not only
     wholesale/trade (批发).
   - Registered capital vs. **paid-in capital** — a large registered figure with negligible paid-in
     capital is a shell.
   - Establishment date, status (存续/在业), and any judicial or abnormal-operation flags.
2. **Export qualification.** Confirm the entity can export in its own name, or identify the agent
   that will issue the export declaration. A mismatch between the shipper on the BL and the
   beneficiary on your remittance creates a FEMA/IDPMS problem at your end.
3. **Physical verification.** Video walk-through of the production line with today's date shown, plus
   a third-party or in-person audit before any tooling payment.
4. **Bank account name must equal the contracting entity name.** Payment to a personal account, a
   Hong Kong intermediary, or a differently-named entity is the single most common fraud pattern —
   refuse it, and treat a mid-order bank-detail change email as fraud until confirmed by phone on a
   previously known number.

## Cluster map

| Cluster | Strength |
|---------|----------|
| Shenzhen / Dongguan / Huizhou | Consumer electronics, PCBA, moulding, chargers, lighting drivers |
| Foshan / Shunde | Hardware, furniture, appliances, sanitaryware |
| Yiwu / Ningbo | Consumer goods, stationery, plastics, small-order aggregation |
| Zhongshan (Guzhen) | Lighting and luminaires |
| Changzhou / Wuxi / Suzhou | Machinery, motors, solar, industrial components |
| Ningbo / Cixi | Electrical accessories, small appliances |
| Shantou (Chenghai) | Toys |
| Quanzhou / Jinjiang | Footwear, sportswear |

Buying inside the right cluster is usually worth more than a hard price negotiation outside it.

## Commercial safeguards

- **NNN agreement** (Non-disclosure, Non-use, Non-circumvention), bilingual, Chinese-law governed,
  with a Chinese court of competent jurisdiction and a stated liquidated-damages figure. An
  English-only US-style NDA is not enforceable in practice.
- **Purchase contract** in Chinese and English, sealed with the company chop (公章) — the chop, not
  a signature, binds a Chinese company.
- **Tooling ownership** stated explicitly, with the right to remove moulds on notice.
- **Quality specification annexed** to the contract: drawings, materials, tolerances, packaging,
  labelling, and the AQL standard that governs acceptance.

## Calendar risk

| Period | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Chinese New Year (Jan–Feb, date varies) | Factories close 2–4 weeks; effective capacity drops from ~3 weeks before |
| Post-CNY ramp-up | Labour turnover; quality dips for 2–4 weeks after reopening |
| Golden Week (early October) | ~1 week closure, port congestion either side |
| Canton Fair (Apr & Oct) | Sales staff unavailable; useful for supplier discovery in person |
| Peak season (Aug–Oct) | Freight rates and space availability deteriorate |

Plan CNY orders to be **shipped**, not just produced, before the shutdown — a finished container
sitting in a closed factory yard is not an asset.
