# Quality Control — AQL Sampling & Inspection Milestones

## Standard

ISO 2859-1 / ANSI-ASQ Z1.4, **General Inspection Level II**, single sampling, normal severity.
Default acceptance limits for consumer goods:

| Defect class | AQL | Meaning |
|--------------|-----|---------|
| Critical | **0** | Safety or legal non-compliance. Any occurrence fails the lot |
| Major | **1.0 – 2.5** | Would cause a return, a complaint, or functional failure |
| Minor | **4.0** | Cosmetic; does not affect function or saleability |

Tighten Major to 1.0 for regulated or safety-relevant goods; 2.5 is the general consumer default.
State the level, the AQLs and the standard in the purchase contract — an inspection report against
an unstated standard settles no dispute.

## Milestones

| Stage | When | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| **FAI** — First Article Inspection | Before mass production | Approve the golden sample against drawings; lock materials, colour, markings |
| **DUPRO** — During Production | At ~20–30% output | Catch a systemic defect while the line can still be corrected |
| **PSI** — Pre-Shipment Inspection | At ≥80% produced and ≥100% packed | The commercial gate: AQL sampling, function tests, carton drop, labelling and barcode check |
| **CLC** — Container Loading Check | At stuffing | Verify quantity, carton condition, load plan, seal number, container cleanliness |

## Non-negotiables

- **Balance payment is released only against a PASS report** from a named third party (QIMA, SGS,
  TÜV, Bureau Veritas, or a verified local inspector). Put this in the contract and in the LC terms
  if using an LC.
- Inspection must verify **regulatory markings** as well as function: ISI/CRS marking where a QCO
  applies, country of origin, MRP/LMPC label content, battery and packaging markings.
- A failed lot means **re-inspection at the supplier's cost**, not a negotiated waiver. Waiving one
  failed PSI teaches the factory the standard is negotiable.
- Retain the approved golden sample at your end, sealed and dated.

## Inspection booking checklist

Product spec and drawings • approved golden sample photos • AQL levels • on-site tests required
(function, dimension, drop, ageing) • carton/labelling artwork • quantity and PO reference •
factory address and contact • date window and access confirmation.
