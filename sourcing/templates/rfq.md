### 3. Information required with your quotation
1. Unit price at each quantity break, MOQ, and production lead time in working days.
2. **HS code** used by your export declaration (6-digit minimum).
3. Country of origin and, if you can certify preferential origin under an FTA with India, the
   origin criterion (WO / CTH / CTSH / RVC%) you would certify.
4. Company registration: registered Chinese name, unified social credit code, business scope.
5. Whether you are the manufacturer or a trading company. If a trading company, name the factory.
6. Existing certifications with certificate numbers and validity (BIS licence number if held).
7. Tooling cost, tooling lead time, and confirmation that tooling is buyer-owned.
8. Carton dimensions, gross weight, and units per 20'/40'HQ container.
9. Bank account details in the **name of the contracting company** (personal accounts not accepted).

### 4. Conditions
- Third-party pre-shipment inspection at AQL Level II (Critical 0 / Major 2.5 / Minor 4.0) is a
  condition of the balance payment.
- A bilingual NNN agreement and purchase contract must be executed before the first PO.
- Any change of bank details must be confirmed by voice call on a previously known number.
