# Pre-Shipment & Pre-Clearance Checklist

Owner: ____________   PO ref: ____________   ETD: ______   ETA: ______

## A. Before the PO
- [ ] 8-digit ITC(HS) confirmed and recorded (source: __________)
- [ ] Import policy checked — Free / Restricted / Prohibited
- [ ] BCD, AIDC, IGST, ADD/safeguard rates looked up; notification numbers recorded
- [ ] Landed cost modelled at MFN **and** at any preferential rate (`tools/landed_cost.py`)
- [ ] Compliance gates cleared (`reference/compliance-gates.md`) — BIS / WPC / LMPC / EPR / FSSAI / CDSCO
- [ ] Supplier corporate record verified on SAMR; bank account name = contracting entity
- [ ] NNN agreement and purchase contract executed with company chop
- [ ] Quality spec, AQL levels and golden sample annexed to the contract

## B. Before production ends
- [ ] FAI approved against golden sample
- [ ] DUPRO booked at 20–30% output (if applicable)
- [ ] Labelling artwork approved: country of origin, importer details, MRP/LMPC, statutory markings
- [ ] LMPC registration in place (pre-packaged retail goods)
- [ ] EPR registration active for packaging / e-waste / batteries as applicable

## C. Before shipment
- [ ] PSI booked and **passed**; report received and reviewed line-by-line
- [ ] CLC done — seal number, container number, load plan photographed
- [ ] Draft documents checked **before** issue: invoice, packing list, BL, CoO
- [ ] CoO type correct (preferential vs non-preferential); origin criterion stated
- [ ] CAROTAR Form I information received where preference is claimed
- [ ] ISPM-15 stamp on wood packaging
- [ ] Freight booking confirms 14–21 free days detention + demurrage in writing
- [ ] Balance payment released only after the above

## D. Before arrival
- [ ] Full document set to CHA ≥72 hours before ETA
- [ ] Advance Bill of Entry filed within the statutory timeline
- [ ] Duty funds arranged; ICEGATE challan ready
- [ ] Non-manipulation certificate obtained for transshipped cargo
- [ ] Delivery order / telex release confirmed with the line
- [ ] Transport and destination warehouse slot booked against the free-time clock

## E. After clearance
- [ ] Out-of-charge obtained; examination findings recorded
- [ ] BoE sent to AD bank; **IDPMS knock-off confirmed in writing**
- [ ] Container returned within detention free time; empty return receipt filed
- [ ] Landed cost actuals reconciled against the model; variance noted
- [ ] Origin and valuation records filed for the statutory retention period
