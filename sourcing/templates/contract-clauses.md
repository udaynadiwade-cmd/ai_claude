# Purchase Contract — Clauses Worth Insisting On

Drafting notes for a bilingual (English/Chinese) supply contract. Have Chinese counsel review the
Chinese text; where the two versions conflict, state which governs.

### Quality and acceptance
> Goods shall conform to the specification, drawings and approved golden sample annexed as
> Schedule A. Acceptance shall be determined by third-party inspection under ISO 2859-1, General
> Inspection Level II, single sampling, at AQL Critical 0 / Major 2.5 / Minor 4.0. A lot failing
> inspection shall be sorted, reworked or replaced at the Seller's cost, and re-inspection shall be
> at the Seller's cost.

### Payment linkage
> The balance payment shall become due only upon issuance of a PASS pre-shipment inspection report
> by [inspection body] and receipt of a copy of the Bill of Lading. Payment shall be made solely to
> the bank account stated in Schedule B, held in the Seller's registered name. Any change to bank
> details shall be effective only after confirmation by voice call to a previously notified number.

### Origin and duty
> The Seller shall state the correct HS classification and country of origin on all shipping
> documents. Where preferential origin is claimed, the Seller shall furnish a valid Certificate of
> Origin and the supporting cost and production records required under CAROTAR 2020 within fifteen
> (15) days of request. Any differential duty, interest or penalty arising from misdeclaration or
> unsubstantiated origin shall be to the Seller's account.

### Regulatory compliance
> The Seller warrants that the Goods and their markings comply with all Indian statutory
> requirements notified to the Seller, including applicable BIS licensing, marking, packaging and
> labelling requirements. Goods rejected or detained by Indian Customs for non-compliance with such
> requirements shall be replaced at the Seller's cost, together with all detention, demurrage and
> re-export costs incurred.

### Delivery and delay
> Time is of the essence. The Seller shall notify any anticipated delay within two (2) working days
> of becoming aware of it. Delay beyond [n] days shall entitle the Buyer to liquidated damages of
> [x]% of the order value per week, capped at [y]%, and to cancel the undelivered balance.

### Tooling and IP
> All tooling, moulds and fixtures paid for by the Buyer are the Buyer's property, shall be marked
> with the Buyer's name, shall be used exclusively for the Buyer's orders, and shall be released to
> the Buyer or its nominee within fifteen (15) days of written demand.

### NNN
> The Seller shall not disclose the Buyer's confidential information, shall not use it other than to
> perform this contract, and shall not sell, offer or supply the Goods or any derivative to any
> customer of the Buyer or into the Buyer's territory. Breach shall attract liquidated damages of
> [amount] per occurrence. Governed by the laws of the PRC; jurisdiction: [named] People's Court.

### Packaging
> The Seller shall use ISPM-15 compliant wood packaging where wood is used, and shall not use
> packaging materials prohibited under Indian regulations. Carton markings shall include the country
> of origin.
